# Interview Defense — Healthcare Commercial Strategy

## 60-second explanation
I built a synthetic healthcare commercial analytics case and then converted the analysis into a consulting-style prioritization problem. Instead of stopping at a dashboard, I asked how a commercial team should allocate limited resources across products, regions, physicians, and patient-continuation initiatives. I structured the problem around market attractiveness, profitability, physician whitespace, and patient continuity, then proposed a weighted prioritization framework and a 90-day pilot with measurable KPIs.

## Questions I should be ready to answer
### Why did you choose these four dimensions?
They cover demand, economics, channel execution, and sustainability of patient reach. Looking at only revenue or only patient reach would give an incomplete recommendation.

### Why use a weighted score?
A score creates a transparent and repeatable decision rule. The weights are assumptions and should be stress-tested with stakeholders.

### What would you change with real company data?
I would add competitive share, market growth, payer or reimbursement constraints, field-force capacity, regulatory restrictions, promotion cost, channel mix, and product lifecycle data.

### What is the biggest limitation?
The dataset is synthetic and observational. The project demonstrates decision structuring, not a real market recommendation.

### What would make the recommendation stronger?
A pilot with pre-defined KPIs, a comparison group, sensitivity analysis on weights, and stakeholder validation from commercial, finance, medical, and compliance teams.
